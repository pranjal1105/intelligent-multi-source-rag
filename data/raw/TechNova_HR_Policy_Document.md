# Chapter 1: Company Overview and Mission

## 5.2 Parental Leave

**Maternity Leave:**
- All female employees who have completed 80 days of service are entitled to **26 weeks (182 days) of paid maternity leave** as per the Maternity Benefit (Amendment) Act, 2017.
- Up to 8 weeks of maternity leave may be availed before the expected date of delivery.
- For adoption of a child below 3 months of age, 12 weeks of paid adoption leave is provided to the adoptive mother.

**Paternity Leave:**
- All male employees and partners (irrespective of gender) are entitled to **10 working days of paid paternity leave** within 6 months of the birth or adoption of a child.
- This is over and above the standard leave entitlement and cannot be combined with EL.

**Miscarriage / Pregnancy Loss Leave:**
- 6 weeks of paid leave in the event of a miscarriage, medical termination, or stillbirth — regardless of gestational age.

# Chapter 6: Performance Management

## 6.1 Performance Review Cycle

TechNova follows a **continuous performance management** approach with formal review touchpoints:

| Review | Frequency | Purpose |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| Check-in Conversations | Monthly | Ongoing feedback, goal alignment |
| Mid-Year Review | October | Progress assessment, course correction |
| Annual Performance Review | March | Final rating, increment and promotion decisions |
| 360° Feedback | Annual | Peer and upward feedback for L5 and above |

## 6.2 Performance Rating Distribution (FY 2024–25)

| Rating | Label | % of Employees |
|--------|-------|---------------|
| 5 | Exceptional | 8.2% |
| 4 | Exceeds Expectations | 24.6% |
| 3 | Meets Expectations | 52.1% |
| 2 | Partially Meets | 11.4% |
| 1 | Does Not Meet | 3.7% |

The company follows a **guided distribution** where the sum of ratings 4 and 5 should not exceed 35% of any team. Exceptions require VP-level approval.

## 6.3 Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)

Employees rated 1 (Does Not Meet Expectations) are placed on a **60-day Performance Improvement Plan.** The PIP includes:
- Clearly defined measurable goals for the 60-day period
- Bi-weekly check-in meetings with HR and direct manager
- Written documentation of all feedback and progress
- At the end of the PIP: exit, extension, or closure with rating upgrade

**PIP statistics FY 2024–25:** 46 PIPs initiated, 18 successfully closed, 21 resulted in separation, 7 extended.

## 6.4 Promotion Process

Promotions are considered annually in March for all bands. Eligibility criteria:
- Minimum 1 year in current level for L1–L4
- Minimum 18 months in current level for L5–L7
- Performance rating of 4 or 5 in the most recent annual cycle
- Vacancy or headcount approval in the target level

**Promotions in FY 2024–25:** 143 employees promoted (11.5% of workforce)

- Company history, vision, values, and culture

# Chapter 10: Code of Conduct

## 10.1 Professional Behaviour Standards

All TechNova employees are expected to uphold the highest standards of professional conduct. The following behaviours are explicitly required:

- Treat all colleagues, clients, and partners with dignity and respect regardless of role, background, or seniority
- Maintain confidentiality of client data, company financials, and internal communications
- Avoid conflicts of interest — disclose to HR any personal financial interest in a client, vendor, or competitor
- Not engage in any form of moonlighting without written approval from the CPO
- Comply with all applicable laws and regulations in every jurisdiction the company operates in

## 10.2 Prohibited Conduct

The following constitute gross misconduct and may result in immediate termination:

- Harassment of any kind — sexual, racial, religious, or based on any protected characteristic
- Theft, fraud, or misappropriation of company property or funds
- Disclosure of confidential company or client data to unauthorised parties
- Violent or threatening behaviour in any workplace (physical, virtual, or online)
- Falsification of timesheets, expense claims, or performance records
- Accessing systems or data without authorisation
- Accepting gifts above ₹5,000 in value from vendors or clients without disclosure

## 10.3 Whistleblower Policy

TechNova maintains a **Whistleblower Hotline** — a confidential mechanism for employees to report any suspected violations of law, policy, or ethics.

- Hotline email: ethics@technovasolutions.com

# Chapter 11: Grievance and Disciplinary Policy

## 11.1 Grievance Redressal

Employees with workplace grievances are encouraged to follow a three-step resolution process:

**Step 1 — Direct Resolution (Day 1–7):** Employee raises the issue directly with the concerned individual or their direct manager. HR is informed but not formally involved.

**Step 2 — Formal Complaint (Day 8–21):** If unresolved, the employee files a written grievance on HRConnect. An HR Business Partner is assigned within 3 working days. Mediation session held within 10 working days.

**Step 3 — Escalation Committee (Day 22–45):** If mediation fails, the grievance is reviewed by the Grievance Redressal Committee — comprising the CPO, a senior HR BP, and one employee representative from a different department. Decision is final and binding.

**Grievances filed FY 2024–25:** 38 (3.1% of workforce)
**Resolved at Step 1:** 14 (36.8%)
**Resolved at Step 2:** 19 (50.0%)
**Escalated to Committee:** 5 (13.2%)

## 11.2 Disciplinary Process

| Severity | Action |
|----------|--------|
| Minor misconduct (1st instance) | Verbal warning, documented |
| Minor misconduct (2nd instance) | Written warning |
| Moderate misconduct | Final written warning + 1-month pay cut |
| Serious misconduct | Suspension pending enquiry + possible termination |
| Gross misconduct | Immediate termination without notice |

# Chapter 12: Remote Work Policy

## 12.1 Work Models

TechNova operates three official work models as of April 2024:

| Model | Description | Eligible Employees |
|-------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Office-First** | 4–5 days per week at office | L1–L3 in first 12 months; all employees on PIP |
| **Hybrid** | 3 days office, 2 days remote | L2 and above with 12+ months tenure |
| **Remote-First** | Fully remote with quarterly in-person sprints | L4+ with manager approval, US/UK hub employees |

- Office-First: 412 employees (33.0%)
- Hybrid: 641 employees (51.4%)

## 12.2 Remote Work Expectations

- Core working hours: **10:00 AM – 6:00 PM IST** (flexible by 2 hours on either side with manager alignment)
- Camera on during client calls and team meetings — no exceptions
- Internet speed minimum: 50 Mbps upload/download; VPN mandatory for all work sessions
- Home office setup allowance: ₹20,000 one-time for employees on Remote-First model (reimbursed on submission of bills)
- Quarterly in-person sprint: 3 days at nearest office — travel and accommodation fully covered by company

## 12.3 International Remote Work

Employees wishing to work from outside India must obtain written approval from both their manager and the CPO at least 30 days in advance. A maximum of 90 consecutive days is permitted per calendar year. Tax and compliance implications are the responsibility of the employee.

## 15.2 D&I Programmes

**Women in Tech Initiative:**
- Mentorship programme pairing 48 women in L2–L3 with L6–L8 mentors
- Return-to-work programme for women after career breaks — 12 hired in FY 2024–25
- Flexible maternity transition support including phased return-to-work over 8 weeks

**Diversity Hiring:**
- Partnerships with 6 universities in Tier-2 cities for campus hiring
- Blind resume screening activated for all positions L1–L4
- Diverse interview panel requirement — at least one interviewer from a different gender or background for every final round

**LGBTQ+ Inclusion:**
- Same-sex partner insurance coverage under group health policy since April 2023
- Gender-neutral washrooms at Bengaluru and Hyderabad offices
- Pride ERG (Employee Resource Group) with 94 active members
- Trans-inclusive leave policy — gender affirmation surgery covered under health insurance
